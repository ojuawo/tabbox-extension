FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did you build 4 different architecture modes instead of just one? +
Every browser user has different screen sizes and habits. On a 13-inch laptop, Chrome's 320px side panel floor wastes huge amounts of horizontal space. The 56px In-Page Rail or the Floating Chat Bubble solves this completely with 0px margin shift. On an ultrawide monitor, the Docked Companion Window or 6-column Bento Grid is king. TabBox lets you switch between all of them on the fly.
Will TabBox slow down my browser or consume tons of RAM? +
No. TabBox is built on modern Manifest V3 standards with a lightweight background service worker that sleeps when inactive. Additionally, TabBox includes a 1-click Tab Snoozer that discards background tab memory without closing your tabs, actively reclaiming RAM.
How does the Floating Chat Bubble launcher work? +
The Chat Bubble is isolated inside a Shadow DOM container on the edge of your screen. Unlike sidebars that push page margins, the Chat Bubble leaves your webpage at 100% full width. Hovering over it (with a 150ms intent debounce) or clicking it slides out the glassmorphic Bento Tab Flyout. You can also drag the bubble vertically up or down to keep it away from site content.
How does the $10 Lifetime License work? +
The $10 Lifetime Deal is a one-time payment powered by ExtensionPay (Stripe). You receive a permanent license key attached to your Google account or email that unlocks all Pro modes on all your computers forever, with no recurring subscriptions.
Is my browsing data or tab history stored on your servers? +
Zero data is ever transmitted to our servers. TabBox operates 100% locally using Chrome's native chrome.storage.local API.
